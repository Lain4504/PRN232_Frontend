# OmniAdly Project Features

## 1. Authentication & Security
- **User Authentication**: Login, Sign Up, Forgot Password, Update Password, Verify Email.
- **Social Login**: Integration with social platforms (e.g., Facebook).
- **Access Control**: Role-based access control for team members and brand access.

## 2. Brand Management
- **Brand Creation**: Create and manage multiple brands.
- **Product Management**: Manage products associated with each brand.
- **Brand Assets**: Manage brand-specific contents and assets.
- **Social Account Linking**: Connect and manage social accounts (Facebook Fanpages) for each brand.

## 3. Content Strategy & Creation
- **AI Content Generator**: Generate social media content using AI assistance.
- **Content Management**: Create, edit, and organize content posts.
- **Content Approvals**: Workflow for reviewing and approving content before publishing.

## 4. Social Media Scheduling & Publishing
- **Post Scheduling**: Schedule content to be published on connected social media accounts (Fanpages).
- **Calendar View**: Visual calendar for viewing and managing scheduled posts.
- **Multi-channel Publishing**: Publish content to multiple social channels.

## 5. Marketing Campaigns
- **Campaign Management**: Create and track advertising, engagement, or awareness campaigns.
- **Campaign Analytics**: Monitor campaign performance (implied by Reports/Dashboard).

## 6. Team Collaboration
- **Team Management**: Create teams and invite members.
- **Permission Management**: Granular permission settings for team members regarding brands and features.

## 7. Subscription & Agency Features
- **Subscription Plans**: Different tiers for users/agencies (Startup, Enterprise, etc.).
- **Agency Tools**: Specialized features for agencies managing multiple clients/brands.
- **Billing & Payments**: Integrated payment processing for subscriptions.

## 8. Analytics & Reporting
- **Dashboard Overview**: High-level view of account performance.
- **Detailed Reports**: In-depth analytics on posts, campaigns, and engagement.

## 9. Public & Support Pages
- **Landing Pages**: Home, Solutions, Features showcases.
- **Resources**: Blog, Documentation (Docs), Pricing, Contact, About Us.
- **Legal**: Privacy Policy, Terms of Service.

## 10. Technology Stack
- **Frontend**: React with Next.js (TypeScript), Tailwind CSS.
- **Backend**: ASP.NET C#, PostgreSQL.
- **Infrastructure**: VPS on Google Cloud.
- **Management & DevOps**: Docker, GitHub.
